Pizza

Pizza is a delicious dish made with a soft dough base, tomato sauce, cheese, and different toppings. It is baked in a hot oven until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted. You can add your favorite toppings to make the pizza your own.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, salt, sugar, and yeast.
  2. Add the warm water and olive oil.
  3. Knead the dough for 5–10 minutes.
  4. Let the dough rest for about 1 hour.
  5. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  6. Roll out the dough.
  7. Add the sauce, cheese, and toppings.
  8. Bake for 12–15 minutes.
