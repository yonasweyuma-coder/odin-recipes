Lasanga

To make a classic homemade lasagna, you first brown ground meat with onions and garlic, then simmer it with crushed tomatoes and seasonings to create a rich meat sauce. Next, mix a creamy cheese layer using ricotta or cottage cheese, parmesan, and eggs. In a baking dish, assemble the dish by alternating layers of meat sauce, lasagna noodles, and the cheese mixture, finishing with a generous topping of shredded mozzarella cheese. Finally, cover the dish with foil and bake at 350°F for about 30 minutes, then uncover and bake for another 15 to 30 minutes until the top is bubbly and golden brown.
