Pasta

Description

Pasta is a simple and delicious meal made with pasta, tomato sauce, vegetables, and seasonings. It is easy to prepare and can be customized with different ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in water until it is cooked.
  2. Prepare the tomato sauce with onion and garlic.
  3. Add salt and black pepper to the sauce.
  4. Drain the cooked pasta.
  5. Mix the pasta with the sauce.
  6. Serve and enjoy.
